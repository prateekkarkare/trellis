# Curriculum — Writing

*The vision and phase map for this domain. Evolves slowly; edited by the mentor when calibration data demands it.*

## Vision

A regular, durable writing practice that produces shippable artifacts (essays, technical posts, fiction drafts) — not a content treadmill. The point is the practice and the artifact, in that order.

## North-star outcomes (1-year)

- 8-12 published essays at the 1500-2500 word range.
- A daily 20-min freewriting habit that survives travel, illness, and busy weeks.
- A working draft of one longer piece (10k+ words) by year-end.

## Phases

### Phase 1 — *Reps without judgment* (weeks 1-6)
Goal: build a frictionless capture habit. Quality bar deliberately low.
- 5x/week 20-min freewriting sessions (any topic, no editing in the same session)
- 1x/week 60-min "essay-shaped" attempt (one idea, beginning-middle-end, doesn't need to be good)
- End-of-week: skim everything written, mark one fragment that surprised you
- Exit: 6 consecutive weeks of ≥4 sessions/week, with at least 3 surprise-fragments

### Phase 2 — *Beginnings and endings* (weeks 7-14)
Goal: stop writing essays that fizzle in the second half. Focus on structural craft.
- Read 1 essay from a sharp writer per week (see intel.md), reverse-engineer its arc
- Take 3 of Phase 1's surprise-fragments and try to shape them into 1500-word essays
- Workshop with one trusted reader per essay before declaring done
- Exit: 3 essays shipped (published anywhere) with explicit feedback that the endings landed

### Phase 3 — *Voice and risk* (weeks 15-22)
Goal: write things only you could have written.
- Identify and abandon 2-3 stylistic tics you're hiding behind
- Write one piece per month that scares you (admits something, takes a stance, embarrasses you slightly)
- Develop a public commitment device (newsletter / blog feed / X account) you actually post to
- Exit: 6 essays shipped under your real name with a subscribed readership ≥50

### Phase 4 — *Long form pilot* (weeks 23-30)
Goal: prove you can sustain a 10k+ word piece.
- Pick one long-form project. Outline. Commit publicly.
- 2-3 sessions/week of dedicated long-form time; freewriting reduced to 3x/week to make room
- Mid-phase: 5k word draft check-in with your trusted reader
- Exit: complete first draft of a long-form piece + retrospective on which habits scaled

## Real-world stakes

- Public commitment: this is mentioned in the year-end review.
- One trusted reader is a real person; this isn't a solo exercise. Their honest feedback is structural.
- The newsletter / blog being public is the difference between a hobby and a practice.

## Cross-domain hooks

- **fitness**: writing happens better post-exercise on observed pattern; coordinator should schedule write blocks for after gym days (see `cross_domain.md`).
- **reading**: every essay in Phase 2's reading list goes into the reading domain's queue too; reading mentor knows.
