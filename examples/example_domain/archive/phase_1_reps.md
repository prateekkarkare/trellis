# Phase 1: Reps without judgment

Domain: writing · Period: 2026-03-25 – 2026-04-12 · Sessions: 3 logged + ~25 unjournaled freewriting reps

## Phase synthesis

Phase 1 did what it was designed to do: built a frictionless 5x/week capture habit without imposing a quality bar. The early friction (week 1: only 2 sessions; week 2: 3) gave way to genuine consistency by week 4 onward (5/week every week from then). The session-anchor pattern (7:30am, kitchen table, paper notebook for first 5 min then keyboard) was the decisive intervention — once that became automatic, the habit held without willpower.

Calibration trajectory: started at 4/10 (mostly logistical setup), drifted to 5/10 by week 3 (the actual writing got slightly harder once the habit reduced novelty), held at 5/10 through phase end. Never approached the 7+ range — which is exactly correct for a habit-formation phase.

What was harder than expected: deciding *not* to edit during freewriting. The pull to revise as you go is strong. Resolution: enforced by physical rule (no backspace key, period). What was easier: the 6-week sustained streak that exit criterion required. By week 4 it was self-sustaining.

What carried into Phase 2: a small reservoir of "surprise-fragments" — passages that came out unbidden and were better than the rest. The grandmother's-kitchen fragment from 2026-04-08 was the first one shaped in Phase 2.
